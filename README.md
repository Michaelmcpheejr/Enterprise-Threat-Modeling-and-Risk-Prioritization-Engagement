# Enterprise Threat Modeling & Risk Prioritization Program

Comprehensive 7-phase security program for federal defense contractors handling Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI).

---

## 7 Phases

**Phase 1: Threat Modeling Foundation & Methodology**  
20+ threat actor taxonomy, asset criticality framework, risk scoring methodology (FAIR-based), framework alignment (NIST 800-171, 800-53, CSF, MITRE ATT&CK)

**Phase 2: Enterprise Asset Inventory & Criticality Assessment**  
100 assets cataloged and scored (62 CRITICAL, 32 IMPORTANT, 6 STANDARD), CUI data flow paths, asset-specific risk indicators

**Phase 3: Enterprise Threat Modeling Scenarios**  
58 threat scenarios mapped, 7 HIGH-RISK scenarios detailed (APT28, Wizard Spider, Sandworm, Lazarus, Insider, Supply Chain), top 10 control gaps identified

**Phase 4: Enterprise Remediation Roadmap & Control Implementation**  
28 primary controls across 4 tiers (Immediate/Urgent/Planned/Baseline), 180-day implementation timeline, cost-benefit analysis, ~5.7:1 ROI, CMMC Level 2 alignment

**Phase 5: Operational Security Controls & Incident Response Planning**  
SOC foundation with 5 detection use cases, 15+ SIEM/EDR rules, 7 incident response playbooks, <24-hour MTTD & <1-hour MTTR targets, staffing model, security metrics

**Phase 6: Governance, Risk Management & Compliance Reporting**  
3-layer governance structure, NIST RMF alignment, CMMC L2 audit readiness, executive dashboards, 7 core security policies, customer compliance reporting infrastructure

**Phase 7: Security Program Maturity & Strategic Evolution**  
Maturity assessment (Level 2.5 → Level 4 over 5 years), competitive benchmarking, talent scaling (3 to 9 FTE), emerging threat strategies, thought leadership pathway
